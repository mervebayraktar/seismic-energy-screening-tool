Decision Support Tool

Seismic screening and energy-efficiency prioritization for public building portfolios

Enter buildings one at a time or import a whole portfolio as CSV or XLSX. The tool computes the Building Score (BS) and Energy Efficiency Score (EES), generates a Prioritization ranking, and keeps the list in the browser, showing how many buildings are loaded, how they are distributed, and which ones should be addressed first.

Stage 1: BS Inputs (Seismic)

The BS step determines the risk range. If the result is red, the EES stage is skipped and Save to Portfolio becomes available immediately so the record can be added as excluded. For yellow or orange, complete Stage 2 first.
